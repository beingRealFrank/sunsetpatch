Scope and price

Every job has a fixed price that we confirm in writing by email before work starts. If the work turns out to be bigger than quoted, we tell you before doing any of it. You can accept the new price, or stop and pay nothing for that part.

Payment

Script jobs are paid on delivery. Connector and library jobs are paid half up front and half on delivery. If we can't deliver what we quoted, we refund what you paid.

Code and access

We work from the code you send or give us read access to. Changes are delivered as a pull request or patch, and nothing merges or deploys unless you do it. We don't need your API private keys. If you choose to give us test credentials, we use them only for the job and ask you to revoke them when we finish.

Your data

We don't sell or share your code or anything about your business. The free check on this site runs in your browser and sends nothing to us.

Fixes

Fixes for anything we changed are free until 30 days after the vendor's cutoff date.
